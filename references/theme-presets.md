# Theme Preset

viaim social cards have one fixed theme: `viaim`.

Read [viaim-brand-system.md](viaim-brand-system.md) for the five exact colors, semantic aliases, contrast limits, light/dark rhythm, and logo constraints.

Use:

```html
<html data-theme="viaim">
```

Do not select or recreate alternate Editorial or Swiss palettes. Structural modes may change typography and composition, but the five-color palette stays fixed.
